# Welcome to the Blogs App

Discover interesting blogs, share your favourites, and keep track of what you want to read.

## Features

- **Browse and search:** Explore <a href="/blogs" aria-label="Browse blogs">blogs</a>, search by title, and like posts you enjoy.
- **Share a blog:** Add a title, author, and link to share a post with other users.
- **Build your reading list:** Save blogs from any user. Blogs you create are saved to your list automatically.
- **Track your reading:** See your Unread and Read lists on [Me](/me), and mark blogs as read when you finish.
- **Explore the community:** Browse [users](/users) and the blogs they have shared.
- **Choose your theme:** Use the sun or moon button in the navigation to switch between light and dark mode.

## Getting started

1. [Create an account](/register), or [log in](/login) if you already have one.
2. [Browse the blogs](/blogs) and open a title that interests you.
3. Select **Add to reading list** on a blog's page to save it for later.
4. Visit [Me](/me) to view your reading list. Select **Mark as Read** when you finish a blog.
5. Have something to share? [Add a new blog](/blogs/new) with its title, author, and URL.

Your profile details and API token are also available on [Me](/me).
